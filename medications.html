15a NAME & TYPE

  • No search: the user types the name as it's on the box.
  • No type is pre-selected: it decides which monthly guide the days count toward, so a default would quietly put days in the wrong place.
  • Next stays disabled until a type is chosen ('Not sure' counts as a choice, under 'Any').
  • Header: Cancel on the right, and the step indicator is the text 'Step 1 of 3' on every step (no dots). Step 1 has no Back.
  • A selected type row shows a check mark on the right, as well as the fill.
  • Disabled Next looks as drawn, but stays focusable and is announced as disabled, with its reason ('Choose a type to continue').
  • Keyboard open: see 15a-3.
  • Heading: 'Add a medicine' is the page heading. 'Step 1 of 3' is plain text, not a heading.

Notes

15b FORM & STRENGTH

  • One fixed list of forms as tiles. '+ Add a different form' as a text action, matching '+ Add a different medication' in the app.
  • Strength is free text, as printed on the box, so it covers combinations ('500/50 mg') and liquids ('100 mg/5 ml'). No unit picker.
  • Totals like '2 tablets · 100 mg' only show when the strength is a single number and unit; otherwise just the count.
  • Header: Back (top left) returns to step 1 and keeps everything entered. Cancel stays on the right. 'Step 2 of 3'.
  • A selected form tile shows a check mark on the right, as well as the fill and border.
  • Next is disabled until a form and a strength are set. It looks as drawn, stays focusable and is announced as disabled.
  • Keyboard open: see 15b-4. 200% text: see 15b-5.
  • Heading: the medicine name is the page heading. 'Step 2 of 3' is plain text, not a heading.
