Reviewing a 113-board redesign

Every board was checked against what the stored data can say, the accessibility floor and the clinical rules before anything was built. Twelve calls needed a decision, plus a batch of smaller token changes. Five of the calls, and the batch:

The board proposed
Decision
Why
A redder high-severity colour
Refused
As text on a raised card it measured 4.20:1, below the 4.5:1 floor the palette holds on every surface. The existing colour stays.
Severity 9
Proposed #D9705F · 4.20:1
Severity 9
Kept #C68880 · 4.72:1
“Did it help? Yes / A bit / No” for every dose
Changed
Kept the board’s words, but worked out from the pain reading about 2 hours after the dose, not asked. One source of truth instead of a verdict beside a number.
Quiet hours, 23:00–07:00
Refused
It would silence reminders during a night attack, exactly when a reading matters most. If it is ever built, it can’t apply while an attack is open.
Purple and blue in the Insights charts
Adopted, with a limit
The app rules out blue and purple for people with light sensitivity. Both colours here are muted, and Insights isn’t a screen anyone opens mid-attack, so the rule bends there and nowhere else.
A five-step end-of-attack flow
Adopted, with conditions
Including its “gone when you woke up” answer, on condition that it’s stored as an unknown end, never a made-up time, and left out of every length figure.
Rounder sheets, a taller footer button, larger toggles
Adopted
Taken screen by screen, each checked in place with its neighbours rather than landed in one blind sweep.

The other seven calls: keep the update-time step · add pain quality, “the two days before” and an under-5-minutes flag · limited editing after the fact · “attack”, never “migraine”, on every screen · keep the attack-mode control · light mode last · keep the settings cog.
