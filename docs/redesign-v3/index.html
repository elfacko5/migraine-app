Today

Builtv3.4

Shipped per CLAUDE.md. Use the screens to check the build matches.

  • Hero: greeting, "[4 days]", "since your last attack". During an attack: label, elapsed time, a pain trajectory line, "[8] now · peak [8] · left side", End attack.
  • Attack mode button sits in the hero's top corner. Its look once switched on is out of scope (see Attack mode).
  • Week card only when 2 or more attacks fall in the last 7 days.
  • 2-hour check-in card after a dose, tied to that dose. No daily check-ins anywhere.
  • Last 30 days, never "this month". T / C / P class bars against 10 / 10 / 15, plus "Any of these" against 10 when more than one class is used.
  • The full build spec is docs/today-v3-handoff.md. The matrix board below says what shows in each state.

The screens show a "headache days" tile and month labels. Both were replaced in the build spec: an attacks tile, and last 30 days. The "How do you feel now?" card after an attack is phase 2 and not agreed yet.

Updating an attack

Not built · conflictsv3.7 + v3.8 + v3.9

Agreed in the design, but it breaks a rule in CLAUDE.md. Ask June before building.

  • Opens on the last reading. Every place from the previous reading is listed with its number. Change only what changed.
  • Each row shows a status: same, better, worse, gone, or new.
  • Tap an existing place: a 0 to 10 picker docks above Save. 0 removes the place ("gone").
  • "It also hurts somewhere else" opens a bottom sheet with the head map. Tap a place, pick its number in the same sheet.
  • Medication and symptoms are part of the same update, each in its own sheet. Medication picks from My medications. Symptoms: only "what bothers you most right now", no ratings.

Conflict: CLAUDE.md says QuickUpdateForm "starts blank, never pre-filled from the previous snapshot". This design carries the last reading forward on purpose, because a blank form can't tell "gone" from "not reported". It also changes what a no_change reading means. Agree the data rules with June first.

Logs list and attack detail

Partly builtv3.10

CLAUDE.md describes some of this. Compare before changing anything.

  • Row: duration as the headline, peak as a shaded number, side glyph on the right. Then day and start time. Then one badge per medication with ✓ helped, – didn't help, or ? no 2-hour reading, and symptom icons. A key under the list explains the marks.
  • "Helped" is the existing rule: pain at least halved or down to 3 or less, at the reading closest to 2 hours. A missing reading is never "didn't help".
  • Detail: tiles for how long, peak and side; a "Pain over time" chart with a dashed line and letter badge at each dose; a card per medication ("after 7h 3m the attack started", "Pain 7 → 3 at 2 hours · helped"); where it hurt at peak and at the end; symptom tiles; the last 3 updates with "Show all"; impact; what was different the day before.
  • Footer: Edit and Add update. Delete moved to a bin icon in the header, still with a confirmation and Undo.

My medications and dose in onboarding

Partly builtv3.9

CLAUDE.md describes some of this. Compare before changing anything.

  • List split into "For an attack" and "Every day". Each row shows its type badge and one dose.
  • Add / edit: name, form, strength, one dose (stepper), type. Limits (max in 24 hours, hours between doses, max days a month) are optional and folded away. Warnings only appear when the user entered a limit.
  • Onboarding asks for one dose of each medicine picked. A known list of six common medicines fills in the type.
  • Delete has Undo.

The design has a fifth type, G (gepant). MedClass has no gepant. Gepants don't count towards medication overuse, so adding one changes the T/C/P counting. Ask June.

Insights: patterns only when there is enough data

Not builtv3.4

Agreed design, not in the app yet.

  • Patterns come only from data collected every day without asking (for example sleep from Health), never from daily check-ins.
  • No pattern before about 10 attacks. Until then the section says how many more it needs.
  • A sleep comparison also needs about 10 nights in the smaller group (for example short nights).
  • Wording states the comparison and stops. No conclusions about causes.

Red flags and age

Not builtv3.2

Agreed design, not in the app yet.

  • Onboarding asks for year of birth, because a new kind of headache that started after 50 needs a doctor. It is about when the headaches started, not the age itself.
  • Two tiers: call 112 now (for example the worst, sudden headache), and see a doctor today. Fever counts only together with a stiff neck.
  • Read the screens for the exact questions and wording. Check every criterion against the dossier before building.
