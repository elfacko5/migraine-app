# Today hero cards — the design calls

`CLAUDE.md` carries the constraints a change has to respect. This is the reasoning behind them, from the 2026-08-14 rebuild and the device pass that followed.

- **The artwork lives in `src/assets/` and is imported, not put in `public/`**, so Vite fingerprints it (`card-ongoing-7HkdvLEt.jpg`). A `public/` file keeps its name forever, and the service worker would happily serve a stale one after an art change. Sources are `ongoing attack card image trimmed.png` (the untrimmed `ongoing attack card image.png` is kept alongside it) / `attack free card image.jpg` in the repo root; the shipped copies are re-encoded to 800px JPEG q65 (1.9 MB → 62 kB for the ongoing one), which is invisibly lossless on flat vector-style art and matters for an offline-first PWA that bundles everything.
- **The gradient is what makes the image a backdrop rather than a panel.** `bg-gradient-to-r` from the card's own `bg-bg-surface` to transparent, weighted left so the text side stays readable. Tune the stops, not the image: this app is read mid-migraine and contrast under the headline beats showing more picture.
- **The gradient's opaque stop must sit *past* the image's left edge.** The image is a hard-edged box (`w-1/2`, right-aligned, so it starts at 50%); wherever the gradient has already begun fading at that edge, flat card colour meets artwork in a single step and a seam runs straight down the card. The opaque stop is 53% for exactly that reason — move the image and the stops move with it.
- **Only the text block is width-limited (`max-w-[64%]`), not the content column.** The buttons need the full width to sit on one row, and being solid shapes they read fine over the artwork where body text would not. At the largest text scale they wrap to a second row and the card grows — that's the intended degradation, not a bug.
- **The headline is `whitespace-nowrap` and overflows that limit on purpose.** "Started 24h 38m" broke onto two lines against the 64% width and pushed the card taller; the headline is the one line that has to land at a glance, and running it over the artwork costs nothing when the buttons already do. Checked at the `xl` text scale, where it stays on one line and still clears the card's right edge.
- **Which edge of the artwork survives the crop is per-card (`imageAnchor`), not a global choice.** `object-cover` always overflows the box horizontally, so one side is trimmed either way, and the right side to keep depends on where the subject sits in that particular source. The ongoing card's art is **pre-trimmed tight to the swirl** (`ongoing attack card image trimmed.png`), so `right` keeps the subject hard against the card edge; the attack-free art is a full square with the moon left of centre, so `left` is what carries the moon into the visible strip instead of burying it under the gradient. Counter-intuitive part worth remembering: keeping the *left* edge pushes a centred subject *right*, because the trim comes off the other side.
- **Spacing is to a supplied spec, and the gaps are deliberately uneven:** 24px left/right and 32px top/bottom padding (`px-6 py-8`), then 4px under the label, 16px under the headline, 24px before the buttons. The label is a kicker attached to the headline, so it sits tight to it; the detail line is a separate fact, so it doesn't. A uniform stack left the label floating between the two. The action buttons are `rounded-xl` (12px at the default text size, and the radius used by ~36 other buttons in the app) rather than `rounded-full`.
- **The image is `alt=""` + `aria-hidden`**: it carries mood, not information, and every fact on the card is in the text beside it.
- **The ongoing card's route to `AttackDetail` is its text block**, via `HomeCard`'s optional `onOpenDetail`. The whole card was the tap target before, which stops working once buttons live inside it (and nesting a button in a button is invalid); a separate "Details" button was tried and dropped as clutter on a card whose job is to be glanceable.
- **Severity is stated plainly as "Pain severity: 9"**, not colour-coded. The old severity-tinted number and border read as part of the artwork once there was artwork behind them, and keeping the detail line the same shape as the attack-free card's is what makes the two interchangeable at a glance.
- `formatSinceLong` ("14 days") backs the headline, separate from `formatSince` ("14d") which is still right for dense rows elsewhere.
