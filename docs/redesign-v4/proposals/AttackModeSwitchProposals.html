Today's attack mode switch — four directions

Today it's a 44 px circle in the hero's top-left corner with the arcs mark and no label (2026-10-28). Two problems with it: the mark has to be learned — nothing on screen says what it does — and "on" is only a sage ring, so once the screen has gone dim and warm there's nothing that reads as "this is why, and here's the way back".

A10 (2026-11-22) kept the circle and backlogged three things from the v4 board: the labelled switch, "turns off again once the attack ends", and a "sensitive to light right now" toggle in logging step 1. These directions take that backlog as the starting point. Each shows the ongoing hero off (normal dark) and on (attack palette, 20 px body).

AThe v4 board's labelled switch

Exactly what the v4 Today boards draw: "Attack mode" with a 40×24 toggle in a 44 px pill, same corner. role="switch", so VoiceOver reads it as a switch rather than a pressed button.

On

For

  • Already designed and decided-in-principle; smallest build.
  • Says what it is. Same switch shape as Settings → Display, so one control, one look.

Against

  • The thing 2026-10-28 removed: "Attack mode" printed above the hero reads as a second headline.
  • On is still just a moved knob — no "why is the screen like this".
  • ~170 px wide at 150% text; needs a check against the art.

BQuiet when off, a clear state when on

Off stays small — the arcs mark plus a short label, no toggle drawn. On, the control turns into a full-width band across the top of the hero: what's on, and a plain way back. The band is the only thing that changes size, so the state is obvious at a glance even with the dim floor applied.

On

For

  • Fixes the real gap: once it's on you can see that it's on and how to undo it.
  • Off is barely louder than today's circle.
  • The band can later carry "Turns off when this attack ends" once auto-off exists (A10 ii) — not shown, as nothing does that yet.

  • The band pushes the hero down ~20 px while on.

COffered when it's needed

With no attack, Today shows nothing — attack mode stays in Settings, and auto-on still fires when one is logged. During an attack with it off, the hero asks once, as a question. Dismissed or turned on, the offer goes and a small "on" chip (as B's off pill, filled) takes the corner.

DOne "screen" control: attack mode and dimming together

The corner circle stays, but opens a small panel instead of toggling straight away: attack mode, the dim slider (today only in Settings), and the auto-on preference. Everything about "make this easier to look at" in one place.

Closed (as today)

Open, attack mode on

For

  • Dimming is the other thing wanted mid-attack, and today it's three taps deep.
  • Labels live in the panel, so the hero stays clean.

Against

  • Two taps to do the main thing, when it's hardest to tap.
  • Doesn't fix discoverability — the closed state is the same unlabelled circle.
  • A new popover pattern; the app has Sheets, not popovers.

My pick: B

The problem worth solving is the on state, not the off one: with the screen dimmed and the text bigger, the person needs to see why and get back in one tap. B does that and leaves the hero's headline alone when it's off. If the label still feels like a second headline, B's off state can drop back to today's bare circle without changing the band. A's switch could replace B's off pill if you'd rather match Settings exactly.

Whichever direction: the first-run TopBar instance follows, and "turns off when the attack ends" needs its own decision — the band is where that line would go.
